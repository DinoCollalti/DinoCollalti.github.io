Hey there! I am a Post-Doctoral Researcher at the [Department of Economics at the University of Bern](https://www.vwi.unibe.ch/index_eng.html) and the [Tourism Research Unit](https://fft.unibe.ch/). You can download my CV <a href="/files/CV_DC.pdf" download>here</a>.

My main research interest is in the effect of tourism, climate, and history on local economic development. Currently, I am studying the consequences of local tourism development in Switzerland via a spatial general equilibrium as well as via a long-run historical perspective. Additionally, I focus on tourism related question such as the price determinants of hotel rooms in Switzerland or the spillover effects of value added in tourism.
